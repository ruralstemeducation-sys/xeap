Đối tượng tham dự

Học sinh được xếp khối theo độ tuổi tính đến ngày 06/05/2027 (ngày kết thúc RECF STEM World Championship 2027):

  • Khối THCS (U15): học sinh từ 15 tuổi trở xuống.

Đề bài 2026–2027: Pinnacle (Đỉnh Cao)

Trận đấu diễn ra trên sa bàn tiêu chuẩn 366 × 366 cm. Robot thu thập, nâng, lồng ghép và xếp chồng các Cốc (Cup) và Chốt (Pin) màu đỏ, xanh dương, vàng lên các Mục tiêu (Goal), xoay các Con lăn (Roller) và đỗ robot tại Bộ nạp (Loader) để tích lũy điểm số tối đa.

Eligibility

Students are grouped by age as of May 6, 2027 (the last day of the RECF STEM World Championship 2027):

  • Middle school (U15): students aged 15 or under.
  • High school (U19): students aged 19 or under who have not graduated from high school.

Each team has one or more students, a valid RECF Achieve team number, and competes with one robot.

2026–2027 game: Pinnacle

Matches are played on a standard 366 × 366 cm field. Robots collect, lift, nest and stack red, blue and yellow Cups and Pins onto Goals, turn Rollers and park at the Loader to score as many points as possible.

60 seconds

Driver Skills

Up to 3 students drive the robot with a controller to score as many points as possible.

60 seconds

Coding Skills

The robot runs entirely on code written by the students.

120 seconds

Alliance matches

15 seconds autonomous and 105 seconds driver-controlled; two teams form an alliance.

Judging

Interview & Engineering Notebook

Teams interview with the judges, who also assess the Engineering Notebook.

Robot rules

QuantityOne robot per team; it must pass Robot Inspection before competing.
Starting sizeFits within a 45.7 × 45.7 × 45.7 cm cube.
ExpansionDuring a match, no more than 61 × 61 cm in length and width; unlimited height.
ControllerIn Vietnam, open robotics platforms are allowed: the controller in the Game Manual, or STM32, ESP32, Arduino, Raspberry Pi…, or a team-designed board. Up to 2 controllers.
Motors · PowerTotal motor power up to 99 W; voltage up to 12 V.
Custom plasticUp to 12 parts (cut sheet ≤ 10.2 × 30.5 cm, ≤ 0.18 cm thick; 3D-printed ≤ 7.6 × 12.7 × 5.1 cm). No acrylic or Plexiglass. Record every part in the Engineering Notebook.
PneumaticsCommercial parts rated for at least 100 psi; maximum charge 100 psi; no on-robot compressor.
License platesTeam number clearly shown on two sides of the robot with red or blue plates matching the alliance colour.
Student-centredStudents design, build, code and explain the robot themselves. Reference designs must be credited and improved by the team.

Format and schedule

Each team must take part in at least one qualifier to be eligible for the National Finals. The season has two online qualifiers (Nov 1, 2026 and Jan 2, 2027) and one in-person qualifier.

  • DEC 11–12, 2026 · HANOIRegional (in person): Driver Skills, Coding Skills, alliance matches, interview and Engineering Notebook.
  • SUBMIT BY DEC 25, 2026 · INTERVIEWS JAN 2, 2027Nationwide online challenge: submit 3–5 skills videos, the Engineering Notebook and an online interview via the submission form.
  • JAN 16–17, 2027 · HANOINational Finals: 5 middle-school (U15) and 5 high-school (U19) spots for international events.
  • FEB 26–27, 2027 · HANOIVietnam International Robotics Tournament (RECF Signature Event): 3 spots for the world finals.
  • APR 27 – MAY 6, 2027 · ST. LOUIS, USARECF STEM World Championship 2027.

Detailed times and venues will be announced by the organizers.
